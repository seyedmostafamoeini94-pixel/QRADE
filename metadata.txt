about=
    QRADE (Risk Assessment for Damage and Exposure) is a QGIS Processing plugin for rockfall risk assessment based on the IMIRILAND methodology for landslides in mountainous areas (Bonnard et al., 2004; Castelli and Scavia, 2008; Scavia et al., 2020). It supports semi-quantitative and quantitative workflows by combining hazard intensity, exposed elements, vulnerability, temporal probability, and the value of the elements at risk.

    QRADE produces GIS risk-assessment layers, summary files, QA/QC reports, an interactive risk dashboard, and BIM-oriented deliverables including BIM-ready exports, IFC GUID mapping templates, and BCF issue files for high-risk and critical features.

    The methodology was developed under the scientific supervision of Marta Castelli. QRADE was designed and implemented by Seyedmostafa Moeini, with technical support from Stefano Campus.

    The development of QRADE was partially supported by PNRR (Piano Nazionale di Ripresa e Resilienza).
